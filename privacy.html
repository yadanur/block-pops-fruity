Privacy Policy

Block Pops Fruity · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Block Pops Fruity is a game for Apple Watch. We care about your privacy, so the game is built to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing. We (the developer) do not collect, store or receive any personal information.

What stays on your watch

Your high scores and your settings (sound on or off, and "Don't show this again" for the tutorial) are saved only on your Apple Watch so the game can remember them. They are never sent to us.

Worldwide leaderboard (Game Center)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score and your Game Center nickname are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on the Block Pops Fruity world leaderboard, where other players can see them. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple ID, email or any other personal information.

You can stop sharing at any time by signing out of Game Center in your iPhone's Settings. The game then keeps working with scores on your watch only.

No tracking, no ads, no accounts

The game has no advertising, no analytics and no sign-up. The only time it connects to the internet is to show and update the Game Center world leaderboard.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from anyone, including children. Game Center features for children are managed by Apple and by parents through Screen Time.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
