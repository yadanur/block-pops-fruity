How to play

  • Drag a piece from the tray at the bottom onto the board.
  • Fill a whole row or column to pop it.
  • Put 3 or more of the same fruit next to each other to pop them too.
  • Clear the entire board for a Perfect Clear bonus.
  • The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

Common questions

How do I turn off sounds and vibrations?
During a game, tap the gear button at the top left and switch Sound (on Apple Watch) or Vibration (on iPhone and iPad) off.

Where are my high scores saved?
Your top 10 scores are saved on the device you play on and stay there even after you close the game. If you use Game Center, your best score also appears on the world leaderboard.

How does the world leaderboard work?
Sign in to Game Center on your iPhone (Settings › Game Center). Your best score then joins the worldwide list, and while you play you'll see your world place climb, like "Globally 8th place!". Open High Scores to see the World list.

Does the watch game need my iPhone or the internet?
No. It runs on its own on your Apple Watch and works offline. The iPhone and iPad versions work offline too. Only the world leaderboard needs a connection and Game Center.

How do I see the tutorial again?
Tap "How to Play" on the main menu.
